About Datihan

Datihan started with a single rack at a weekend market and a simple idea: good clothes, shoes, and pants shouldn't sit unworn just because they're not new. No storefront, no overhead — just honest pricing and pieces worth digging for.

We buy, consign, and thrift pants, shirts, and shoes, then bring them to pop-ups around the city and keep the full catalog online in between. Follow along to know where we'll be next.
